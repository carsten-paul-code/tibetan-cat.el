# Common Tibetan Vocabulary
# Everyday words for narrative and descriptive texts
# Format: Tibetan[TAB]English[TAB]Notes

# === BODY PARTS & ACTIONS ===
སྙིམ་པ	handful	cupped hands; amount held in cupped hands
ལག་པ	hand
རྐང་པ	foot/leg
མགོ	head
མིག	eye
རྣ་བ	ear
སྣ	nose
ཁ	mouth
ལྕེ	tongue
སོ	tooth
སྤུ	hair (body)
སྐྲ	hair (head)

# === WATER & LIQUIDS ===
ཆུ	water	also: river
ཆུ་བོ	river
མཚོ	lake
རྒྱ་མཚོ	ocean	great lake
ཆུ་ཀླུང	stream
འཐུང	drink	to drink
འཁྲུ	wash	to wash
རླན་པ	wet/moist

# === VERBS OF TAKING/GIVING ===
བཅུས	scooped	past of འཆུ་བ: to scoop/gather
འཆུ་བ	to scoop	to gather with hands
ལེན	take	to take/receive
བླངས	took	past of ལེན
སྟེར	give	to give
བྱིན	gave	past of སྟེར (honorific: གནང)
འབུལ	offer	to offer/present
ཕུལ	offered	past of འབུལ

# === QUANTITY & MEASUREMENT ===
གང	full/one	amount that fills; which?/what?
ཕྱེད	half
ཐམས་ཅད	all
མང་པོ	many
ཉུང་ངུ	few
ཆུང་ངུ	small
ཆེན་པོ	big/great

# === DEITIES & BEINGS ===
ལྷ	deity/god
ལྷ་མོ	goddess
ཀླུ	naga	water spirit/serpent deity
གནོད་སྦྱིན	yaksha	nature spirit
དྲི་ཟ	gandharva	celestial musician
མི་འམ་ཅི	kinnara	half-human celestial
ལྷ་མ་ཡིན	asura	demigod/titan
བདུད	mara	demon/evil one

# === NARRATIVE CONNECTORS ===
དེ་ནས	then	after that
དེ་ལ	to that/there
དེ་ལྟར	thus/so	in that way
འདི་ལྟར	thus/like this	in this way
ཅི་སྟེ	if/what if
གལ་ཏེ	if	conditional
ན	when/if	locative or conditional particle

# === HONORIFIC VOCABULARY ===
གསུང	say (hon.)	honorific for speak
གསོལ	request/eat (hon.)	honorific request; honorific for eat
གཟིགས	see (hon.)	honorific for look
བཞུགས	stay/sit (hon.)	honorific for dwell
མཛད	do (hon.)	honorific for act
བཀའ	word/speech (hon.)	honorific for speech/command
ཞབས	feet (hon.)	honorific for feet
ཕྱག	hand (hon.)	honorific for hand
དབུ	head (hon.)	honorific for head
སྐུ	body (hon.)	honorific for body

# === COMMON VERBS ===
བྱེད	do/make	present stem
བྱས	did/made	past stem
བྱ	will do	future stem
བྱོས	do! (imperative)
འགྲོ	go	present/future
སོང	went	past
ཕྱིན	went (hon.)	past honorific
འོང	come	future
ཡོང	came	past (in some dialects)
ཤོག	come! (imperative)
བལྟ	look	present
བལྟས	looked	past
བལྟ་བ	to look	infinitive
སྡོད	stay/sit
བསྡད	stayed	past
འདུག	is/exists	existential (witnessed)
ཡོད	is/exists	existential (general)
རེད	is	copula (Lhasa)
ཡིན	is	copula (identity)

ངན་པ	bad/evil

ནག་པོ	black
དམར་པོ	red
སེར་པོ	yellow
སྔོན་པོ	blue/green
རིང་པོ	long
ཐུང་ངུ	short
ཞི་བ	peaceful
དྲག་པོ	fierce/wrathful
